Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026. This policy covers the NextMode Instagram integration, operated by Asaf Shimon Magen for @nextmode.il. It is separate from the personal Google assistant described elsewhere on this domain.

Information processed

Depending on granted permissions and the task, the integration may access the managed account's identifiers and username, published media and captions, comments, interaction information, and message conversations. Messages may include sender identifiers, usernames, timestamps, text and attachments made available by Meta. Access does not extend to your password or unrelated private conversations.

The integration uses this information to manage the account, prepare and publish approved content, understand incoming requests, respond where permitted, prevent duplicate actions, and investigate technical failures. API messaging is currently being configured and tested; this policy does not mean all functions are available.

Processing and service providers

The operator's self-hosted agent environment processes account information. Relevant message text or summaries may be sent to the configured AI service to assist with a reply, and may be shared with the operator through Telegram for review. Meta provides Instagram and API access. Email providers process privacy requests sent by email. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

NextMode does not sell account interaction data. Private messages are not intentionally published on this information website. This static website has no signup form or analytics scripts added by NextMode; its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request data such as IP addresses under its own policies.

Storage and security

Credentials, conversation history, operational records, logs and backups may be stored in the operator's environment. There is currently no automatic fixed retention schedule for all records. Information can remain in logs or backups until the operator removes it. Access is limited to the operator and the services needed for the task. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, verification codes, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive information in messages. Automated responses may be wrong; request review by Asaf where needed.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may stop messaging the account or use Instagram's blocking and privacy controls. The account owner can revoke the application's access through Instagram's Apps and Websites settings. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by NextMode, contact asafmagen28@gmail.com. Follow the data deletion instructions.

Removing NextMode's own records does not automatically delete records independently held by Instagram, other service providers or another participant. This policy will be updated when data handling changes.
