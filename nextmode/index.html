NextMode Instagram integration

Operated by Asaf Shimon Magen for @nextmode.il. The integration manages this account; it is not a service that connects other businesses' Instagram accounts.

What the integration does

The account operator uses Meta's Instagram API and authorized account tools to publish approved content and manage account interactions. API messaging is under setup and testing. When available, it will be used to read incoming messages and respond within Meta's permitted messaging windows. Automated assistance does not authorize unsolicited bulk messages.

The account shares practical content about AI, careers, psychology and habits. This website provides information about the integration and does not provide a login or collect Instagram passwords.

Your information

See the privacy policy for how account interactions may be processed and the data deletion instructions to make a request. You can ask for the account operator, Asaf, to review a conversation rather than continue with automated assistance.
