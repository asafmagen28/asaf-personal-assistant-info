Data deletion instructions

For information processed by the NextMode Instagram integration for @nextmode.il.

Send a request

  1. Email asafmagen28@gmail.com with the subject "NextMode data deletion".
  2. Include your Instagram username and specify whether you want all information held by NextMode about your interaction removed or only a particular conversation or record.
  3. If needed, the operator may ask you to confirm the request from the same Instagram account. Do not send a password, access token, verification code, or identity document.

Requests are handled manually by Asaf Shimon Magen. You can use the same email address to ask for the status of your request. This page does not submit a request by itself.

What removal covers

The operator reviews records under NextMode's control, including saved conversation extracts and operational records associated with the identified account, and removes the requested information where applicable. If removal cannot be completed in full, the operator explains the limitation. Backup copies and records independently held by service providers may require separate handling; immediate deletion from all backups is not promised.

A request to NextMode does not automatically erase Instagram's copy of the conversation or copies held by another participant. Use Instagram's own controls or contact the relevant provider for their records. See the privacy policy for processing details.

Disconnect the integration

The owner of @nextmode.il can revoke access in Instagram's Apps and Websites settings. Revoking access stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically delete records previously stored in the operator's environment.
