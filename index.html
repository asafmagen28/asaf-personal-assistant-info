Asaf Personal Assistant

A private assistant for one account owner. It organizes information, answers questions, and supports personal routines. The assistant itself is not available for public registration or use.

What it connects to

With the owner's authorization, the app can access Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs and read-only Contacts to assist with email, scheduling, documents and personal organization. It can also read Google Health information, including data from a connected Fitbit device, for personal health summaries. The precise permissions are displayed in Google's consent screen.

The app runs in the owner's self-hosted agent environment. Specialized assistants for health and personal finance may use direct, owner-authorized connections; actions such as sending mail or changing calendar events require the owner's approval.

Information and control

Access is granted through Google OAuth. The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings. Data is handled as described in the Privacy policy. This page does not contain personal health records, messages or credentials.
